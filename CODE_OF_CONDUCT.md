# Community conduct

Nova Flux welcomes useful questions, reports, and contributions from people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to this
repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, explain disagreements with
evidence, and give others room to learn. Respect privacy, authorship, and licenses.
Do not post harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, personal
attacks, spam, or another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock a discussion, or
restrict participation according to the conduct and its impact. A correction or
request to stop should be taken seriously. Enforcement is contextual; no fixed
response time or appeals service is promised.

Use GitHub's **Report content** control for abuse, including a short factual
description and relevant links. Do not create a public issue containing sensitive
details or repeat the harmful material unnecessarily. Security vulnerabilities
use the separate [private security route](SECURITY.md).

This is the project's own conduct policy, not a claim of affiliation with an
external enforcement organization.
